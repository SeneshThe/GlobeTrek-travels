About Ella Adventure

Experience the natural beauty of Ella with breathtaking mountain views, scenic railway journeys and unforgettable adventures. This package is designed for travelers who want to explore the peaceful hill country while experiencing some of Sri Lanka's most popular attractions.

Package Highlights

Nine Arches Bridge

Enjoy an amazing hike and panoramic mountain views.

Ravana Falls

Explore one of the most popular waterfalls in the region.

Scenic Train

Experience Sri Lanka's famous hill-country train journey.
